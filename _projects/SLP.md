<h4><b>Overview</b></h4>
In this work, I approached spoken language processing as an end-to-end systems problem rather than a collection of isolated models. The project spanned phonetic transcription and audio analysis, speech synthesis and transcript cleanup, conversational ASR with CTC-based neural models, and adaptation of base speech models to multilingual settings. Working across that range made the interfaces between components impossible to ignore: alignment, transcript quality, decoding choices, domain mismatch, and evaluation all mattered as much as the architecture itself.

I was responsible for the full workflow from exploratory analysis and implementation through training, fine-tuning, and error analysis. The main lesson I carried forward was practical: strong spoken-language products are rarely defined by one model decision alone; they depend on disciplined data handling, clear task framing, and evaluation under realistic conversational conditions.
